    17 years in GIS and geospatial, from leading GEOINT teams in the US Army to building Amazon Corporate Security's GIS programme and now leading GIS at Ridge.

    Built environment consultancy

    2025 – present

    Ridge and Partners LLP, Newcastle upon Tyne

    GIS Solutions Architect (Senior Associate), Digital Engineering

    Ridge and Partners LLP · Newcastle upon Tyne · September 2025 to present

    • Oversees GIS solutions across Ridge from strategy to delivery as part of the Digital Engineering team; administers the firm's ArcGIS Online organisation and sets its GIS standards.
    • Guides clients through critical GIS decisions and shapes digital estate roadmaps that move organisations from fragmented data to confident, evidence-led planning.
    • Leads end-to-end delivery of interactive 2D and 3D estate experiences across healthcare, higher education and housing, uniting drone, survey, BIM and asset data in a single view: BIM and floor plan pipelines, and the orthomosaic, 3D scene and defect mapping deliverable on drone condition surveys.
    • Builds and scales Ridge's GIS offer, including Experience Builder web applications, Survey123 and Field Maps mobile applications, and floor-aware mapping for capacity management.

    Security intelligence and enterprise GIS

    2017 – 2020 · 2022 – 2025

    Amazon, London and Prague

    GIS Solutions Architect

    Amazon · London · August 2023 to September 2025

    • Technical lead for GIS within Amazon Corporate Security, guiding architecture, platform and tooling decisions.
    • Led the design and development of the programme's GIS infrastructure, migrating it from ArcGIS Online to ArcGIS Enterprise hosted on AWS, and architected platforms that replaced multiple legacy solutions, delivering more than £2m in cost savings.
    • Developed and deployed AI-powered features using AWS Bedrock and SageMaker, integrating large language models and machine learning into the platform.
    • Trained corporate intelligence teams in Seattle, Dubai and London through hands-on GIS workshops.

    GIS Manager

    Amazon · London · April 2022 to August 2023

    • Rejoined Amazon to establish and lead a new GIS programme: built it from the ground up, defining strategy, priorities and roadmap, and secured buy-in across Corporate Security by turning operational needs into the programme's first GIS use cases.
    • Launched Corporate Security's ArcGIS Online environment and managed its budget and licensing, setting the standards and governance that allowed it to scale.

    Senior Risk and Resilience Intelligence Analyst

    Amazon · Prague · February 2017 to June 2020

    • Led data-driven risk analysis and incident response using geospatial tools and statistical modelling, and built automated monitoring systems to identify and mitigate operational vulnerabilities across Amazon facilities.
    • Developed real-time incident management dashboards and analytical frameworks that improved response times during critical events.
    • Designed and delivered training programmes, technical documentation and hands-on workshops across Amazon sites globally.

    Military geospatial intelligence

    2008 – 2015

    United States Army, global operations

    Operations Manager and Senior Geospatial Intelligence Analyst

    United States Army · Global operations · June 2008 to July 2015

    • Directed daily operations, training and professional development for a team of up to 60 personnel while serving as Senior GEOINT Analyst supporting United States Special Operations Command (SOCOM) and Special Operations Command Africa (SOCAF).
    • Led geospatial intelligence production for special operations forces, NATO and national-level agencies, overseeing more than 1,400 imagery products and over 1,100 hours of full motion video analysis.
    • Brought GIS into frontline intelligence, maintaining daily route layers of IEDs, checkpoints and points of interest, and fusing imagery with signals and human intelligence into a single operational picture.
    • Held a Top Secret/SCI clearance with polygraph and completed specialised training including DGS-SOF, AFSOC Full Motion Video analysis certification and Joint Special Operations Task Force certification.
