Case studyRidge and Partners LLP · 2025 – present

Drone condition survey mapping

Condition surveys delivered as a map instead of a spreadsheet: an orthomosaic, a 3D scene and every defect in its place, found in high-resolution drone imagery.

Role
Lead for the ArcGIS deliverable
Teams
Geospatial and Building Surveying
Platform
ArcGIS

In [ ]:
cs.before()

Before

A condition survey deliverable was often an Excel sheet: a list of defects and their descriptions.

A list tells a client what is wrong, but not where on the building it is, and it gives them nothing to navigate or share with the people who will fix it.

cs.workflow()

Workflow

  1. 01 · Capture

    Drone flight

    • High-resolution inspection imagery
    • Captured with the Geospatial team

  2. 02 · Process

    Imagery products

    • Orthomosaic
    • 3D scene

  3. 03 · Inspect

    Defects found

    • Surveyors review the inspection imagery
    • Defects identified and recorded

  4. 04 · Deliver

    Client package

    • Web app with mapped defects
    • 3D scene
    • Report

Diagram of the workflow, not client data.

How defects are captured

Surveyors find defects by reviewing the high-resolution inspection imagery from the drone capture, rather than in person. Each defect is recorded against the imagery, so it lands on the map where it actually is.

In [ ]:
cs.outcome()

Outcome

  • Faster turnaround

    Clients receive the mapped survey sooner.

  • Better coverage

    More of the building is surveyed, from imagery the drone captures.

  • Easier sign-off

    Defects can be seen and checked in context, not read from a row in a spreadsheet.

  • Safer

    Surveyors inspect from imagery instead of in person on site.
