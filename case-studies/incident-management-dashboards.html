Case studyAmazon · 2017 – 2020

Real-time incident management dashboards

Real-time dashboards and automated monitoring for the incidents that affect operations: weather, security, supply disruption and cargo crime.

Role
Senior Risk and Resilience Intelligence Analyst
Based
Prague
Tools
R, ArcGIS Dashboards, Tableau, QuickSight

In [ ]:
cs.context()

Context

Risk and resilience work depends on knowing what is happening, where, and what it touches.

The job was to turn incident data into something teams could act on during critical events, and to watch for risk automatically rather than waiting for it to be reported.

In [ ]:
cs.workflow()

Workflow

  1. 01 · Watch

    Incident data

    • Weather
    • Security
    • Supply disruption
    • Cargo crime

  2. 02 · Analyse

    Spatial and statistical

    • Geospatial analysis
    • Statistical modelling in R

  3. 03 · Monitor

    Automated rules

    • Vehicles entering high-risk areas
    • Checks run automatically

  4. 04 · Act

    Alerts and dashboards

    • AWS SNS alerts
    • ArcGIS Dashboards
    • Tableau and QuickSight

Diagram of the workflow, not operational data.

Example: cargo crime

One monitor watched for cargo trucks entering areas with a high risk of cargo crime. When a truck crossed into one, an alert went out through AWS SNS, so the right people knew in real time.

In [ ]:
cs.outcome()

Outcome

  • Faster response

    Teams saw incidents as they happened, improving response times during critical events.

  • Fewer missed events

    Automated monitoring meant fewer events went unnoticed.

  • Wider coverage

    Weather, security, supply disruption and cargo crime watched across Amazon facilities.
